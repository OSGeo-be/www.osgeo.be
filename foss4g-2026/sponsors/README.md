## Step-by-step workflow

### Step 1 — Prepare the contact list

- Previous sponsor list: Excel file on the [Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dj_Q9ymCPM8VxsDllkAdzvWb0uUDz5fD?usp=drive_link) (sent by Gaël to Xavier)
- Review and update: remove duplicates, add new prospects
- Identify the right contact person at each organisation

### Step 2 — Send the email campaign (Mailchimp)

- **Access:** [us7.admin.mailchimp.com](https://us7.admin.mailchimp.com) — credentials in **KeePass** (Drive → KeePass file, master password required)
- **Campaign name:** `FOSS4G Belgium 2026 — Call for Sponsors`
- **Template:** HTML file available on the Drive — import via *Campaigns → Create → Code your own*
- **Content:** trilingual (EN / FR / NL) — see [Email content reference](#email-content-reference) below
- **Audience:** use the sponsors list (not the general mailing list)
- Send, then note the send date in the tracking sheet

### Step 3 — Follow up

- Wait **5–7 business days** after the email
- If no reply: follow up by phone or personal email
- Log all interactions in the tracking sheet on the Drive

### Step 4 — Negotiate / custom packages

- All discussions via [board@osgeo.be](mailto:board@osgeo.be)
- Custom options: social event sponsorship, year-round support, etc.
- Confirm the chosen tier and any specific arrangements in writing (email is fine)

### Step 5 — Confirm the sponsor

- [ ] Collect the logo (PNG/SVG, high resolution)
- [ ] Collect the invoice details (company name, address, VAT number if applicable)
- [ ] Confirm the tier and amount by email
- [ ] Log in the tracking sheet (sponsor confirmed ✅)

### Step 6 — Publish the logo on foss4g.be

- Add the logo file to the GitHub repo (open a PR or ask Gaël / Marc)
- Update the sponsors page on [foss4g.be](https://www.foss4g.be)
- Gold sponsors: also add to the header banner

### Step 7 — Invoice & payment

- Issue the invoice from OSGeo Belgium (Gaël handles this)
- Payment to the OSGeo Belgium bank account
- Log payment received in the tracking sheet

### Step 8 — Communications

Once the sponsor is confirmed, trigger the relevant communications:
